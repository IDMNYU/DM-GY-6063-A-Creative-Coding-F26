## Self portrait

Using a still image, array of images, recorded video, or live webcam feed, capture your face with the camera and manipulate it. Iterate over the pixel array and ask a question about the values within. can you highten your sense of self? Fade into the bacground? How can you toy with the image to make yourself appear different in the video space than in the real world?

Try and incorporate interface objects, the mouse, or keyboard so that you can chaneg parameters dynanically and on the fly.
