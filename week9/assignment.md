## Gestural control

### Machine learning with ml5.js
Using one of the ml5.js models, write a sketch that enables you to control parameters with your body — this could be tracking your face, hand, or skeleton. Think about the position of the camera and what it captures. Will you show the body or outline of control points? What information will you control or change? How do the gestures and positions you're tracking map to the experience?

In addition to the sketch, write up your thoughts on the questions above in the README or inline in the index page of your sketch.
